# Scalloped edge

The crimped edge used on the pierogi bags, as a strip 8 in wide with 35 scallops along the bottom, in each flavor color plus cream.

- `.svg`: best for Canva (stays sharp at any size, and Canva lets you change its color).
- `.png`: transparent background, for apps that don't take SVG.

In Canva: Uploads > Upload files, pick the file, then drag it onto the design and stretch it across. Use Flip > Flip vertical to put the scallops on top.
