Pierogi boxes, z wzorem ludowym

Your navy-and-cream Canva layout, redrawn with Bolesławiec pottery and Wycinanki paper cuts. The pierogi sits on a stoneware plate, trees of life stand on either side, and two paper-cut birds face the logo. Each filling keeps the same box and changes one accent color.

What comes from where

  • Bolesławiec pottery: the cobalt-on-cream palette, the peacock-eye dots (pawie oczko) around the plate rim, and the dotted scallops at the band edges.
  • Wycinanki paper cuts: flowers built from stacked layers of color, mirrored left and right, plus the crowned tulips, rosettes and facing birds.
  • Northside: the red logo in a cream roundel, the ink-drawing pierogi from the website (redrawn in blue), Fraunces and EB Garamond, and the "since 1992" tagline.

Before it goes to print

  • Net weight reads "00 oz (000 g)". Put in the real weight. US labels need it on the front.
  • Logo is the small screen PNG. Swap in the vector original for print.
  • Flavor colors (violet, mustard, green, tomato) are outside the red-and-cream brand guide. They appear only as small accents, as on painted pottery.
  • The back panel needs ingredients, allergens, cooking steps and a barcode.
