Northside Bakery · frozen pierogi · box fronts, 6 × 10 in

Four boxes, four paintings

Each filling has its own painted Polish folk scene in an arched window, with a plate of pierogi on Bolesławiec stoneware in front. They're made like art prints you'd want to keep, and each box has its own bold color so the four look good together on a shelf or in a freezer door.

Folk details in the paintings

  • Blueberry: a girl in a Łowicz striped skirt, coral beads (korale) and a flower wreath with ribbons, picking jagody under a crescent moon.
  • Potato & Cheese: a Zalipie cottage painted with flowers, a stork nesting on the chimney, a smiling folk sun over flowering potato rows.
  • Sauerkraut & Mushroom: birch and spruce forest in autumn, cabbages, porcini and chanterelles, and a hedgehog carrying a mushroom.
  • Meat: a Wycinanki-style rooster on a fence at sunset, a wooden village church, poppies, cornflowers and rye.

Before it goes to print

  • Net weight reads "00 oz (000 g)". Put in the real weight. US labels need it on the front.
  • Logo is the small screen PNG. Swap in the vector original for print.
  • Box colors (rose, teal, burgundy, cobalt) go beyond the red-and-cream brand guide. Add them to the guide as a packaging palette if you keep them.
  • The back panel needs ingredients, allergens, cooking steps and a barcode.
