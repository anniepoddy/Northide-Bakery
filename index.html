What we're known for

Five favorites from the counter, made right here every day. The Polish name is underneath, with how to say it.

  • Polish donuts

    Pączki

    Say it: POHNCH-kee

    Pillowy, filled and glazed. By the dozen on Fat Thursday.

  • Handmade pierogi

    Pierogi

    Say it: pyeh-ROH-gee

    Pinched by hand, sold by the piece. Eight fillings.

  • Cabbage rolls

    Gołąbki

    Say it: gaw-WOHMP-kee

    Rice and meat wrapped in cabbage, hot from the food bar.

  • Cheesecakes

    Sernik

    Say it: SEHR-neek

    Made with farmer's cheese. Plain, with fruit, or Oreo.

    Blintzes

    Naleśniki

    Say it: nah-lesh-NEE-kee

    Thin crêpes rolled around sweet cheese, apple, or cheese and fruit.

    A family bakery since 1992

    Three brothers, a sister and their parents. Still family, still on North 8th.

    Richard and Teresa Podedworny opened Northside Bakery in 1992 in Greenpoint, for a neighborhood that missed the taste of home. Within a year the bakery had outgrown its space and moved to the North Side of Williamsburg, which gave us our name.

    The family put their talents together and earned "The Best Bread in Brooklyn." We still run the bakery today.

    The neighborhood has changed a lot since then, and so have we. Alongside the rye and the pączki you'll now find a hot food bar, American cakes and a market full of European groceries. And the stoop out front is still where the neighborhood likes to sit.
